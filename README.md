# Land Maps

Offline city maps for land scouting. Open the app, download a city once, and it stays on your device.

**App:** https://adngraph220.github.io/land-maps/

- On iPad/iPhone: open in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**
- Each city is a pack under `cities/<id>/` (map sheets + `pack.json` with highlighted areas)
- Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors · OpenFreeMap · OpenMapTiles
